snipr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

snipr does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data, anywhere, ever.

  • No network access. snipr makes no network connections of any kind. It has no analytics, no telemetry, and no crash reporting that leaves your Mac.
  • Everything stays on your Mac. Screenshots you capture are processed and saved entirely on your device, to a folder you choose yourself.
  • On-device text recognition only. snipr's sensitive-content check (flagging emails, phone numbers, API keys, or card numbers in a capture) runs using Apple's on-device Vision framework. The image and any recognized text never leave your Mac.
  • No third-party SDKs. snipr contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking libraries of any kind.
  • Preferences only. The only data snipr stores is your own app preferences (such as your chosen save folder), kept locally via standard macOS preferences storage.

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.

Questions? See the Support page.
